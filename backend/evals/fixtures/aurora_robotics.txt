Aurora Robotics Overview

Aurora Robotics is a fictional company founded in 2019 that builds warehouse
picking robots. Its flagship product is the AR-7 arm, which can pick up to
900 items per hour. The company is headquartered in Fictionville and was
founded by Maya Chen and Tomas Ruiz. Aurora Robotics raised a Series B round
of 40 million dollars in 2022, led by Fictional Ventures. As of the most
recent fictional annual report, the company employs 140 people across three
sites.

The AR-7 arm uses a six-axis design and a suction-and-pinch gripper that
switches automatically depending on the shape of the item being picked. It is
rated for continuous operation in refrigerated warehouses down to minus 20
degrees Celsius, which is the feature most often cited by customers in the
cold-storage sector.
