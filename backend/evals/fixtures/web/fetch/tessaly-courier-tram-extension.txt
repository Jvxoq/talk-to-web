Council backs Brindlewick tram extension to Verrin Quay

Tessaly Regional Council voted on Tuesday evening to extend the Brindlewick tram line by four stops, ending at Verrin Quay. The vote passed after a debate that ran past nine o'clock. Councillor Odalys Pemberton said the extension would replace the 41 bus on the same route.

Supporters told the chamber that the 41 bus is often late and full at rush hour. A few councillors worried about the cost of laying new track along the harbour road. The council said it would pay for the work from its transport fund.

Work is due to start after the winter break. The council will publish the timetable once the contractor is chosen. Riders can expect a short bus replacement service while the new stops are built.
