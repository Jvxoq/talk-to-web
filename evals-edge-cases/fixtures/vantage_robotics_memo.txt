Vantage Robotics Internal Memo

Vantage Robotics is a fictional maker of loading-dock automation. Its current
product is the VR-4 pallet mover, which can carry loads up to 1200 kilograms
and navigates using floor-mounted magnetic tape. The VR-4 was first shipped
in 2021, and the current fleet software version is 3.4.

The VR-4's magnetic tape guidance was chosen over LIDAR because tape is
cheaper to install in an existing warehouse and does not need recalibration
after a shelving change. Vantage Robotics was founded in 2020 by Priya Nair.

Fleet software version 3.4 added collision-avoidance braking, which stops the
VR-4 within 0.3 meters of an obstacle at full load. This is the feature most
requested by warehouses that share floor space with forklifts.

Vantage Robotics employs 60 people at a single site and has not raised
outside funding, choosing instead to grow from customer revenue.
