Nimbus Instruments is a fictional maker of rooftop weather stations, and this

The NW-3 measures wind speed and direction with an ultrasonic anemometer,
model UA-40, which has no moving parts. It also records rainfall with a
tipping bucket gauge and reports every reading over a LoRa radio link to an
indoor base unit called the Nimbus Hub.

The NW-3 is powered by a small solar panel and a backup battery that keeps it
running for several days without sun. Mount it at least two meters above the
roof surface, well away from chimneys and air vents, because warm exhaust air
spoils both the temperature and the wind readings.
